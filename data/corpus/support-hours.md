# Customer Support

Customer support is available Monday through Friday from 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time. Support is closed on public holidays and during the winter break from December 24 to January 1. The fastest way to reach support is live chat in the app. Phone support has an average wait time of about 8 minutes. Email replies usually arrive within one business day. Support handles billing, delivery and returns questions but cannot change prices after an order is placed.
