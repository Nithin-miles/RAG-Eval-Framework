# Payments

We accept Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards, PayPal and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery, bank transfers or cryptocurrency. Payment is charged when the order ships, not when it is placed. If a payment fails, the order stays in Payment Pending for 24 hours before it is cancelled automatically. Refunds always go back to the original payment method. Gift cards can be combined with a credit card on a single order.
