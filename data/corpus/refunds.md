# Refund Policy

Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase, but only for unused items in their original packaging. The customer must provide proof of purchase, such as an order confirmation email or receipt. Once a refund is approved, the money is returned to the original payment method. Refund processing takes 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received. Shipping charges are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged. Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable.
