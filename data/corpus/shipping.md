# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days and is free on orders over 50 dollars. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs an additional 15 dollars. Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time ship the same business day. We currently deliver only within the United States and do not offer international shipping. Delivery to Alaska and Hawaii takes 2 extra business days. Tracking details are emailed once the carrier scans the parcel.
